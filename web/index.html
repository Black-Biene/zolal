Zolal

Hide files inside photos, videos, songs and PDFs.

Hide files or text inside an ordinary photo, video, song or PDF. You get back a file that still opens normally.

  1. 1 Choose a cover file

  2. 2 Add what to hide

    Choose a cover file first.

  3. 3 Set a passphrase

    You'll need it to get everything back. It can't be recovered.

Send the result as a file, not as a photo or video.

WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and most chat apps shrink photos and videos when you send them, and that deletes what's hidden. Songs and PDFs are safest sent as files too. Send it as a document/file (WhatsApp: attach → Document; Telegram: attach → File), by email, or inside a .zip.

Open a file that has something hidden in it, using the passphrase it was hidden with.

Everything happens on your device. Your files and passphrase are never uploaded; we don't receive or store anything. Once this page has loaded it keeps working with the internet off.
