Zolal

Hide files inside photos, videos and PDFs.

Pick a cover file and what to hide in it. You get back a file that still opens normally.

You'll need it to get the files back. It can't be recovered if you forget it.

Send the result as a file, not as a photo or video. WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and most chat apps shrink photos and videos when you send them, and that deletes what's hidden. Send it as a document/file (WhatsApp: attach → Document; Telegram: attach → File), by email, or inside a .zip.

Open a file that has something hidden in it, using the passphrase it was hidden with.

Everything happens on your device. Your files and passphrase are never uploaded; we don't receive or store anything. Once this page has loaded it keeps working with the internet off.
