TrustMark test: hide a short text in a photo, read it back with the camera.

Experiment. Runs Adobe's watermark models on this device; nothing is uploaded. The first use downloads them (hiding ~65 MB, reading 90–130 MB).

  1. 1 Choose a photo

    Busy photos (trees, streets) hide the mark best.

  2. 2 Text and password

    up to 32: letters, digits, space, “.”

    Up to 6 characters use one mark over the whole photo, the sturdiest. Longer texts use four marks, one per quarter; any one quarter may be unreadable.

Send it as a file or a normal photo, show it on a screen or print it; then read it in Reveal.

Scan it like a QR code: hold the phone close, so the picture fills most of the photo.
